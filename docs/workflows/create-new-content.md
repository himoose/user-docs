# Create New Content

## What this helps you do

This is the workflow recipe for **new content** — instead of reacting to a visibility drop, it proactively scans your tracked topics on a schedule and stages new content for the sub-topics you don't cover yet.

## Building the workflow

The workflow reads as one sentence, built from these choices:

> **On a** [daily / twice daily / weekly / bi-weekly / monthly] **basis, find new sub-topics under** [all topics / a specific tracked topic], **and create a content brief, a first draft, and up to** [1–5] **posts, then** [notify me / publish (coming soon)].

- **Cadence** — how often this workflow scans for content gaps.
- **Topic scope** — all your tracked visibility topics, or just one.
- **Post count** — how many brief-plus-draft sets to generate per run, up to 5.
- **Final action** — **notify me** when new drafts are ready for review. **Publish** is planned but not available yet.

## Deep research on paid managed plans

On a **Paid Managed** workspace, this workflow automatically includes deeper competitor and answer-engine research in every brief and draft it generates. On Preview Mode and BYOK plans, you'll see a note that upgrading to a paid managed workspace unlocks this automatic deep research — the workflow still runs and generates content without it, just without that extra competitive grounding step.

## How it runs

Unlike Recover Lost Visibility, this workflow can be triggered on demand: click **Run now** to run it immediately using your current settings, in addition to its normal schedule. If new sub-topics worth acting on are found, Hi, Moose tells you and links straight to your [Inbox](../inbox/overview.md); if nothing new turns up, it tells you that too rather than staging filler content.

## Where the results go

Every run's briefs and drafts are staged as real [content briefs and drafts](../features/content-briefs.md), reviewable the same way as anything else generated by Hi, Moose — nothing is published without your review, even with "publish" selected once that option ships.
